What we actually do

Nine services. One case file.
No hand‑offs.

Most agencies pass you between three different people and a WhatsApp group. One officer owns your file here, from consultation to arrival.

Most requested

Admissions & school placement

We shortlist schools against your grades, budget and the visa route you actually qualify for — then write, submit and chase the applications until an offer letter lands.

  • Course and school shortlisting with real entry requirements
  • Statement of purpose and reference letters, drafted with you
  • Transcript evaluation, WAEC/NECO and WES where needed
  • Scholarship and tuition-discount applications
Where files fail

Visas & immigration filing

Student, visitor, work and dependant routes. Every document is reviewed in-house against the current caseworker guidance before it goes anywhere near a portal.

  • Route eligibility assessed honestly, before you pay a filing fee
  • Full document pack assembled, checked and indexed
  • Biometrics and appointment booking
  • Interview coaching and refusal-appeal support

Accommodation abroad

A signed tenancy or a confirmed hall place before you board — never a "we'll sort it when you land".

  • Student halls, private flats and shared houses
  • Deposits and guarantor problems solved
  • Short-let cover for your first two weeks

Flights & ticketing

IATA-standard fares, student baggage allowances and dates that line up with your registration deadline.

  • One-way, return and open-jaw student fares
  • Extra-baggage negotiation
  • Travel insurance and airport transfer

IELTS, TOEFL & PTE prep

Small classes and mock tests until your band score clears the requirement, not just the average.

  • Diagnostic test before you register
  • Weekend and evening cohorts
  • Test-centre booking handled for you
Done properly

Proof of funds & financial documentation

The single most common reason a Nigerian application is refused. We show you exactly what the caseworker needs to see, how long it must sit there, and which sponsor letters actually hold up — using your own genuine funds.

  • Maintenance-requirement calculation per country
  • Bank statement and sponsorship letter formatting
  • Tuition deposit and SEVIS/CAS payment guidance
  • Domiciliary account and FX transfer routes

Arrival & settling in

The first fortnight is where people struggle. We stay on the file until your bank account opens and your registration is confirmed.

  • Airport pickup and first-night accommodation
  • Bank account, SIM card and transport card setup
  • School registration and residence-permit collection
  • Introduction to the local Nigerian student community
